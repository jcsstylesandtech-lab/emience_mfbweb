How to Open an Account

Open Your Personal Account in 5 Simple Steps

Opening an account with Eminence Microfinance Bank is quick and straightforward. Follow these steps, and our team will help you if you need it.

  1. Choose Your Account

    Pick the account that suits you: Savings, Current, Youth or Women's Banking.

  2. Get Your Documents Ready

    Have your BVN, NIN, a valid ID and a passport photograph ready.

  3. Start Your Application

    Apply on the Eminence Mobile Banking app, or visit our Kubwa office.

  4. Verify Your Identity

    We confirm your BVN, NIN and details, as required by Nigerian KYC rules.

  5. Fund & Start Banking

    Receive your account number, make your first deposit and start transacting.

What You'll Need

  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
  • National Identification Number (NIN)
  • Valid IDNIN slip, international passport, driver's licence or voter's card
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Active phone number and email address
  • Utility billIf required for your account level (proof of address)

Requirements can vary by account type. Our team will confirm exactly what you need.
