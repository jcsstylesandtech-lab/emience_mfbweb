How do I open a personal account?

Follow the bank's official account-opening process and provide the identification and verification information required for your account type.

What documents are required?

Requirements depend on the account type and applicable KYC rules. Confirm the current requirements through the bank's official channels.

Can I open an account online?

Online account opening may be available for eligible customers if the bank's official digital onboarding platform supports it.

How do I access Internet Banking?

Use the bank's verified Internet Banking link and follow the official authentication process.

Are there account maintenance fees?

Applicable fees depend on the account and service. Review the bank's current approved tariff before opening an account or completing a transaction.

How do I reset my PIN or password?

Use the official account-recovery process or contact verified customer support.
