How to Open a Business Account

Get Your Business Banking in 5 Steps

Here is how to open your Eminence business account, from gathering your documents to your first transaction.

  1. Choose Your Account

    Choose a Business Current Account, a Business Savings Account, or both.

  2. Prepare Your Documents

    Gather your CAC documents, TIN, proof of address, and the owner's ID, BVN and photograph.

  3. Submit Your Application

    Visit our Kubwa office with your documents, or request a call from our business team.

  4. Verification & Approval

    We complete KYC checks on the business and its signatory, then approve the account.

  5. Activate & Transact

    Get your account number, set up the Eminence Business app and request POS if you need it.

What Your Business Will Need

  • CAC documentsCertificate of Business Name Registration and Status Report from the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
  • IdentificationValid government-issued ID for the owner/signatory: international passport, National Identity Card/NIN slip, driver's licence or voter's card
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)BVN of the business owner or sole signatory
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN)Company or individual TIN linked to the business
  • Proof of addressRecent utility bill (electricity, water or waste bill) from the last 3 months
  • Passport photographRecent passport-size photograph(s) of the signatory
