Ask Our Team

Product availability, Sharia approval, terminology and governance details are confirmed by the Bank before any product is offered. We do not claim that the Bank or all its products are Sharia-certified without supporting evidence.

What is Sharia-compliant banking?

It refers to financial services structured in accordance with applicable Islamic finance principles and the relevant product's approved governance arrangements.

How is Islamic financing different from conventional loans?

The structures may differ depending on the product. The bank will explain the specific arrangement, total cost, payment obligations, and applicable principles.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Requirements depend on the product, customer category, and applicable verification process.

Can businesses access Islamic financing?

Eligible businesses may apply for products offered by the bank, subject to availability and approval.

Are there any fees?

All applicable charges and obligations are disclosed in the product documentation.
