1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your computer, phone or tablet when you visit it. They help the website work properly, remember your choices and, with your permission, understand how the website is used.

We also use similar technologies, such as the browser storage our offline feature uses to keep copies of our pages and images on your device. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of these.

2. How We Use Cookies

Eminence Microfinance Bank Limited ("Eminence", "the Bank", "we", "us") uses cookies on this website to:

  • Keep the website secure and working properly.
  • Remember your cookie preferences, so we don't ask you on every visit.
  • Load extra features you choose to use, such as the Google map on our Contact page.
  • With your permission, understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it.

We ask for your consent before placing any cookie that isn't strictly necessary, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary cookies

These are needed for the website to work and can't be switched off. They don't store personal information you would recognise, such as your name.

Functional cookies

These enable extra features. For example, the embedded Google map on our Contact page is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies. We only load the map once you've allowed functional cookies or chosen to show it.

Analytics cookies

These would help us understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are most popular. We don't currently use analytics cookies. If we start, they will only run once you've accepted them.

Marketing cookies

These would help us show relevant Eminence messages on other websites. We don't currently use marketing cookies. If we start, they will only run once you've accepted them.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by other companies whose services appear on our website, such as Google Maps. We don't control these cookies. Please see the relevant company's own privacy and cookie policies, for example the Google cookie policy.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit our website, a banner lets you accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or choose which types to allow.

You can change your mind at any time: open Cookie Settings, or use the "Cookie Settings" link at the bottom of every page. We ask you again every 6 months, or sooner if the cookies we use change.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the website from working properly.

7. Changes to This Policy
