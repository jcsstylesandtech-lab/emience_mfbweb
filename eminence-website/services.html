How It Works

Four Simple Steps

  1. Choose Your Purchase

    Select an eligible product or service from a participating merchant.

  2. Apply for Approval

    Provide the required information for an eligibility assessment.

  3. Review Your Payment Plan

    Understand the payment schedule, total amount payable, applicable fees, and conditions before accepting an offer.

  4. Pay According to the Agreed Schedule

    Make payments on time in accordance with your approved agreement.

Buy Now, Pay Later is not yet an active product. It will only be offered once the Bank confirms availability, operating model, regulatory compliance, merchant arrangements, eligibility, fees, repayment terms and any applicable Sharia-compliance arrangements.
