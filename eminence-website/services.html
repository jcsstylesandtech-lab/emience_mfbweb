Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL)

Get What You Need Today. Plan Your Payments Responsibly.

The proposed Buy Now, Pay Later service is intended to help eligible customers access selected goods or services and spread payments over an agreed period, subject to approval and the applicable terms.

  • Convenience when purchasing eligible goods and services
  • Clear payment schedules
  • Better planning of planned expenses
  • Responsible financing arrangements
Make an Enquiry Contact Us

How It Works

  1. Choose Your Purchase

    Select an eligible product or service from a participating merchant.

  2. Apply for Approval

    Provide the required information for an eligibility assessment.

  3. Review Your Payment Plan

    Understand the payment schedule, total amount payable, applicable fees, and conditions before accepting an offer.

  4. Pay According to the Agreed Schedule

    Make payments on time in accordance with your approved agreement.

Buy Now, Pay Later is not yet an active product. It will only be offered once the Bank confirms availability, operating model, regulatory compliance, merchant arrangements, eligibility, fees, and repayment terms.
