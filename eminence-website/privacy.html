1. Introduction

Eminence Microfinance Bank Limited (“Eminence”, “the Bank”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our customers, prospective customers, and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, open or operate an account, use our digital banking services, or interact with us through our customer support channels.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection laws and other relevant regulatory requirements.

By using our website or banking services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain it before processing your personal information on that basis.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information, depending on the services you use and applicable legal requirements.

2.1 Personal Identification Information

  • Full name.
  • Date of birth.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address.
  • Residential address.
  • Nationality and relevant identification details.
  • Valid identification documents.

2.2 Banking and Financial Information

  • Bank Verification Number (BVN).
  • National Identification Number (NIN).
  • Account details and account type.
  • Transaction history and payment records.
  • Beneficiary and recipient information.
  • Source-of-funds information, where required.
  • Information relating to savings, financing, cards, and other banking products.

2.3 Device and Technical Information

  • IP address.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Login records and access timestamps.
  • Website usage information.
  • Security logs and diagnostic data.

2.4 Communications and Customer Support Information

Information you provide when contacting us, submitting enquiries, reporting complaints, requesting assistance, or communicating with our representatives.

2.5 Verification and Compliance Information

Where necessary, we may obtain or verify information through authorised identity-verification providers, credit-reference agencies, payment providers, regulatory databases, and other lawful sources.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for legitimate banking, operational, security, and regulatory purposes, including:

  • Opening, verifying, and managing customer accounts.
  • Providing banking, savings, payment, transfer, card, and financing services.
  • Processing transactions and maintaining accurate records.
  • Verifying customer identities and preventing impersonation.
  • Meeting Know Your Customer (KYC), anti-money laundering, and counter-terrorism financing obligations.
  • Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraud.
  • Responding to customer enquiries and resolving complaints.
  • Sending transaction alerts, account notifications, and service updates.
  • Improving our website, digital platforms, and customer experience.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, tax, and reporting obligations.
  • Sending marketing communications where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.

We process personal information only where an applicable lawful basis exists.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may process personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Contractual necessity: To establish or perform our banking relationship with you.
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable banking, financial, tax, and data protection laws.
  • Legitimate interests: To protect our systems, prevent fraud, improve services, and manage operations, provided your rights are appropriately respected.
  • Consent: Where you have freely given consent for a specified purpose and consent is the appropriate legal basis.
  • Other lawful grounds: Where processing is permitted under applicable law.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal and may not prevent processing required on another lawful basis.

5. BVN, NIN, and Identity Verification

To comply with applicable identification and banking requirements, we may request your BVN, NIN, valid identification documents, residential address, and other relevant verification information.

We may submit or compare these details through authorised verification systems and service providers where legally permitted.

You are responsible for providing accurate, complete, and up-to-date information. Inaccurate or incomplete information may prevent us from providing certain services or may result in account restrictions where necessary and lawful.

We apply appropriate safeguards to protect identification information against unauthorised access, disclosure, or misuse.

6. How We Share Your Information

We may share personal information where necessary and lawful with:

  • Regulatory and government authorities: Including the Central Bank of Nigeria, the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation, and other competent authorities, where applicable and legally required.
  • Identity-verification providers: To verify BVN, NIN, identification documents, and customer details.
  • Payment and financial service providers: To facilitate transfers, card transactions, bill payments, and related services.
  • Technology and hosting providers: To maintain our website, digital banking systems, security infrastructure, and support platforms.
  • Professional advisers and auditors: Where necessary for lawful business, audit, compliance, or legal purposes.
  • Law enforcement and courts: Where disclosure is required or permitted by law.
  • Other authorised recipients: Where you have provided appropriate authorisation or another lawful basis permits disclosure.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, we take appropriate steps to require suitable confidentiality, security, and data protection measures.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

Depending on the system and information involved, these measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption, system monitoring, confidentiality procedures, fraud detection, incident-response processes, and security assessments.

No website, digital platform, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage customers to protect their login credentials and report suspicious activity promptly.

8. How Long We Retain Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including account administration, transaction processing, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and compliance with legal and regulatory obligations.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, the nature of the banking relationship, applicable recordkeeping requirements, and whether a legal claim or investigation requires continued retention.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to securely delete it, destroy it, or anonymise it, subject to applicable law.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, maintain security, remember preferences, understand website usage, and improve user experience.

Where required by law, we will request consent before placing non-essential cookies or using similar technologies.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings or through a cookie preference tool provided on our website. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

Where applicable, our Cookies Policy provides further information about the cookies we use and how to manage your preferences.

10. Marketing and Communications

We may contact you through email, SMS, telephone, or digital notifications for service-related purposes, including transaction alerts, security warnings, account updates, and important notices.

Where permitted by law, we may also send information about our products, services, and relevant offers.

You may opt out of marketing communications using the unsubscribe instructions provided or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing will not prevent us from sending essential service, security, transaction, or legally required communications.

We will respect your applicable privacy and communication preferences.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any relevant exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Request a copy of eligible personal information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Some requests may be limited where we are required to retain or process information to meet legal, regulatory, security, or contractual obligations.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a privacy request.

12. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

We maintain procedures intended to identify, assess, and respond to personal data breaches.

Where a breach occurs, we will assess the incident and take appropriate remedial action in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements, including notifying the relevant authority and affected individuals where required.

Customers should promptly report suspected unauthorised access, compromised credentials, or suspicious transactions through our verified customer support channels.

13. International Data Transfers

Where personal information needs to be transferred to or accessed from another country, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that the transfer complies with applicable Nigerian data protection requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include assessing the destination's level of protection, implementing appropriate safeguards, or relying on another legally recognised transfer mechanism.

14. Children's Privacy

Our banking services are subject to applicable account eligibility requirements. Where we offer products or services involving children or persons under 18, we will process their personal information in accordance with applicable legal requirements and any necessary parental or guardian authorisation.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for purposes that are unlawful or incompatible with the service being provided.

15. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, applications, payment services, or other external platforms.

Those third parties may operate under their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services, except to the extent required by applicable law.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, operational practices, or legal and regulatory requirements.

Where appropriate or legally required, we will notify customers of material changes through our website, email, SMS, mobile application, or other suitable communication channels.

The latest version will be published on our website with its effective or updated date.
